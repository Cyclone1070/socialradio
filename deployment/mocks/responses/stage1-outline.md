# Segment Outline: The Rent Dilemma

## STEP 1 - Cold Open
- Dave teases tonight's dilemma: a caller whose landlord keeps the deposit.
- Sarah promises a concrete answer before the break.

## STEP 2 - The Caller's Story
- Caller explains the flat, the move-out date, and the missing deposit.
- Dave asks for the one detail everybody forgets: the check-in report.

## STEP 3 - The Pushback
- Sarah plays devil's advocate for the landlord's side.
- Caller admits the report was never signed.

## STEP 4 - The Verdict
- Dave gives the practical steps: photos, written demand, small claims.
- Sarah closes with the deadline the caller must not miss.
